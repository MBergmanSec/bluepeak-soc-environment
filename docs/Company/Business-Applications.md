# Business Applications

## Purpose

This document provides an overview of the core business applications used throughout BluePeak Logistics. These systems support the organisation's day-to-day operations and enable employees to perform their roles efficiently.

BluePeak follows a hybrid application strategy, using cloud-hosted services where practical while retaining selected on-premises systems that support core business operations and integrations.

---

# Hybrid Application Strategy

BluePeak uses a hybrid application model.

Where practical, business applications are delivered as cloud services to reduce infrastructure management and improve scalability.

On-premises infrastructure is reserved for identity services, business integrations, security monitoring, file services and other systems that provide operational value by remaining under BluePeak's direct control.

---

# Business Critical Applications

The following systems are considered critical to business operations:

- Active Directory
- Microsoft 365
- Warehouse Management System
- Transport Management System
- Integration Platform
- Splunk Enterprise
- File Services

Extended outages affecting these systems would have a significant impact on business operations and require immediate attention.

---

# Application Ownership

Each business application has an assigned business owner responsible for operational requirements and business processes.

Technical administration remains the responsibility of the IT and Security teams where applicable.

This separation ensures business decisions and technical decisions remain clearly defined.
