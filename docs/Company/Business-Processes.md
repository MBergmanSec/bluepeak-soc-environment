# Purpose

This document provides an overview of BluePeak Logistics' primary business process.

Understanding how work flows through the organisation helps employees understand how departments collaborate to deliver services, and provides context for business operations, technology, and cybersecurity.

---

# Business Model

BluePeak Logistics is a hybrid logistics company providing warehousing, inventory management, and transportation services for commercial customers across Australia.

The company stores customer inventory within its distribution centres and manages the movement of goods from storage through to final delivery.

---

# Primary Business Process

The core business process of BluePeak Logistics is Order Fulfilment.

```text
Customer
    │
    ▼
Customer Service
Receives customer request
    │
    ▼
ERP System
Sales order created
    │
    ▼
Warehouse Operations
Pick and pack inventory
    │
    ▼
Transport & Fleet
Schedule delivery
    │
    ▼
Customer
Receives goods
    │
    ▼
Finance
Issue invoice and receive payment
```

---

# Department Involvement

| Department | Role |
|------------|------|
| Customer Service | Receives customer requests and creates sales orders. |
| Warehouse Operations | Picks, packs and prepares inventory for dispatch. |
| Transport & Fleet | Schedules deliveries and manages transport operations. |
| Finance | Issues invoices and processes customer payments. |
| Information Technology | Supports the systems used throughout the process. |
| Security Operations | Protects business systems and assists during security incidents affecting business operations. |

---

# Business Systems

The Order Fulfilment process is supported by several core business systems that enable departments to work together efficiently.

| Process Stage | Department | Primary Business System |
|---------------|------------|-------------------------|
| Customer Request | Customer Service | Customer Portal / CRM |
| Sales Order | Customer Service | Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) |
| Inventory Management | Warehouse Operations | Warehouse Management System (WMS) |
| Delivery Scheduling | Transport & Fleet | Fleet Management System (FMS) |
| Invoicing & Payment | Finance | Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) |

# Critical Business Systems

The Customer Portal, Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system, Warehouse Management System (WMS), and Fleet Management System (FMS) are considered critical business systems because they directly support BluePeak Logistics' Order Fulfilment process.

Disruption to any of these systems may significantly impact daily operations and the company's ability to deliver services to customers.

# Business Impact

Order Fulfilment is considered a critical business process.

Disruptions affecting any stage of this process may result in:

- Delayed customer deliveries
- Financial loss
- Customer dissatisfaction
- Operational disruption
- Reputational damage

# SOC Perspective

Understanding the Order Fulfilment process helps Security Operations assess the business impact of security incidents.

By identifying which stage of the process has been affected, analysts can prioritise investigations, determine the urgency of response activities, and communicate operational impact to business stakeholders.
