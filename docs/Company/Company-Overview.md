# 3. Company Overview

BluePeak Logistics is a privately owned Australian logistics and warehousing company headquartered in Brisbane, Queensland.

Founded more than 20 years ago as a regional freight business, BluePeak has grown steadily through long-term customer relationships, strategic expansion, and continued investment in its people and operations. Today the company employs over 300 staff across multiple locations throughout Queensland and New South Wales.

BluePeak provides freight transportation, warehousing, inventory management, and distribution services for a diverse range of commercial customers. The company supports time-sensitive supply chains where reliable technology and secure business operations are critical to maintaining customer confidence.

As a logistics provider, disruption to technology can directly impact freight movements, warehouse operations, customer deliveries, and business continuity. Protecting business systems is therefore essential to maintaining reliable operations and customer trust.

As the organisation has grown, so has its technology environment. BluePeak now operates a mixture of modern cloud services and established on-premises infrastructure that reflects its gradual expansion over many years. While the company continues to modernise its systems, maintaining the stability, availability, and security of existing services remains a key business priority.

BluePeak's continued growth has been built upon several core principles that guide both business operations and customer relationships.

- Reliability – Delivering dependable logistics services for customers.
- Safety – Protecting employees, customers, and the communities in which we operate.
- Continuous Improvement – Investing in people, technology, and business processes.
- Long-Term Partnerships – Building lasting relationships with customers, suppliers, and employees.

# 4. Our Business

BluePeak's core services include:

- Freight transportation
- Warehousing and distribution
- Inventory management
- Customer logistics services
- Supply chain support

Reliable technology enables every part of these operations. Employees depend on business systems to communicate, manage inventory, coordinate deliveries, process payroll, and support customers across all company locations.

# 5. Technology Environment

Technology is a core business function at BluePeak Logistics.

Business operations rely on a combination of Microsoft cloud services and on-premises infrastructure to support employees across all locations.

The technology environment has developed gradually alongside the company's growth. As a result, the organisation maintains a balanced mix of modern platforms and legacy systems while following an ongoing programme of continuous improvement.

Detailed information about infrastructure, systems, and architecture is maintained within dedicated technical documentation.

# 6. Security and Your Role

Security is a shared responsibility across BluePeak Logistics.

Whether your role is in IT, infrastructure, networking, or security operations, protecting company systems and customer data forms part of everyday business operations.

Technical employees are expected to:

- Follow company security policies.
- Report suspicious activity.
- Protect company information.
- Support the availability and reliability of business systems.
- Contribute to continuous improvement within their area of responsibility.

The Security Operations team works closely with Information Technology and business stakeholders to detect, investigate, and respond to security events while supporting the organisation's operational objectives.

# SOC Perspective

Understanding the business is the foundation of every investigation.

Security incidents cannot be assessed in isolation. Knowing what BluePeak does, how it operates, and how technology supports the business allows analysts to better evaluate business impact, prioritise investigations, and communicate risk effectively.

Strong technical investigations begin with understanding the organisation they are protecting.
