# Purpose

This document provides an overview of BluePeak Logistics' office and operational locations across Australia.

It explains the purpose of each site and how the company's expansion has supported business growth over time.

---

# Company Growth

BluePeak Logistics was established in Brisbane over 20 years ago.

As the company expanded, additional distribution centres and regional offices were opened to support growing customer demand across Australia.

This gradual expansion has resulted in a combination of modern and legacy facilities, reflecting the company's long-term growth and continuous investment in its operations.

# Organisational Notes

- Brisbane serves as the company's corporate headquarters.
- Major business systems are managed centrally by the Information Technology department based in Brisbane.
- Regional locations support local operations while relying on centralised corporate services.
- Older regional sites may operate with a mixture of modern and legacy technology due to the company's gradual expansion.
