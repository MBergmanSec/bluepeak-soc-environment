# Purpose

This document provides a high-level overview of BluePeak Logistics' organisational structure.

It is intended to help employees understand the company's executive leadership, reporting relationships, and departmental ownership. Detailed department responsibilities are documented separately in **Departments.md**.

---

# Executive Organisation Structure

```text
Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
│
├── Chief Operating Officer (COO)
│   ├── Warehouse Operations
│   ├── Transport & Fleet
│   ├── Customer Service
│   └── Procurement & Supply Chain
│
├── Chief Financial Officer (CFO)
│   └── Finance
│
├── Chief Information Officer (CIO)
│   ├── Information Technology
│   └── Security Operations
│
├── HR Director
│   └── Human Resources
│
└── Marketing Manager
    └── Marketing & Communications
```

---

# Executive Leadership Summary

| Position | Primary Responsibility |
|----------|------------------------|
| Chief Executive Officer (CEO) | Provides overall leadership, strategic direction, and governance for BluePeak Logistics. |
| Chief Operating Officer (COO) | Oversees logistics operations, warehousing, transport, procurement, and customer service. |
| Chief Financial Officer (CFO) | Responsible for financial management, budgeting, reporting, and compliance. |
| Chief Information Officer (CIO) | Oversees information technology, cybersecurity, and business technology strategy. |
| HR Director | Leads recruitment, employee relations, workforce planning, and human resource management. |
| Marketing Manager | Responsible for brand management, communications, and marketing initiatives. |

---

# Organisational Notes

- The Chief Executive Officer (CEO) has overall responsibility for BluePeak Logistics.
- Department managers and operational teams are not shown in this overview.
- Security Operations and Information Technology operate as separate functions under the Chief Information Officer (CIO).
- Executive leaders are responsible for the strategic direction of their respective departments.

# SOC Perspective

Understanding the organisational structure allows Security Operations to identify business ownership during investigations, determine appropriate escalation paths, and assess potential business impact when incidents affect specific departments.

# Acceptance Criteria

After reading this document, an employee should be able to:

- Identify BluePeak's executive leadership structure.
- Understand which executive owns each department.
- Recognise where Information Technology and Security Operations fit within the organisation.
- Understand that detailed departmental responsibilities are documented separately.
