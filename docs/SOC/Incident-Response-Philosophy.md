# Incident Response Philosophy

## Purpose

This document outlines the principles followed by the BluePeak Logistics Security Operations team when investigating security incidents.

The objective is to ensure investigations are consistent, evidence-based and focused on supporting business operations.

---

# Investigation Philosophy

BluePeak investigations are guided by the following principles:

- Evidence over assumptions
- Business context before technical detail
- Facts separated from hypotheses
- Confidence based on available evidence
- Clear communication
- Practical recommendations

Every investigation should provide enough information for another analyst to understand what occurred, how conclusions were reached and what actions are recommended.

---

# Investigation Process

Security investigations generally follow five stages.

## 1. Understand the Incident

Establish what has been reported, which systems are involved and the potential business impact.

---

## 2. Gather Evidence

Collect relevant information from available telemetry, business documentation and supporting systems.

---

## 3. Develop Hypotheses

Consider multiple possible explanations for the observed activity before reaching a conclusion.

Alternative explanations should be eliminated using available evidence wherever possible.

---

## 4. Reach a Conclusion

Document conclusions using objective language supported by evidence.

Where evidence is insufficient, clearly state any uncertainty.

---

## 5. Recommend Actions

Provide practical recommendations appropriate to the level of confidence and business impact.

Recommendations may include:

- Continue monitoring
- Escalate the incident
- Contain affected systems
- Request additional investigation
- Close the incident

---

# Business Impact

Technical activity should always be considered within the context of the business.

The same technical event may have very different priorities depending on the affected system, user or business process.

Business impact forms an important part of every investigation.

---

# Confidence Assessment

Every investigation should include a confidence assessment.

Confidence reflects the quality and quantity of available evidence rather than certainty.

Confidence Levels:

- High
- Medium
- Low

A lower confidence assessment does not indicate poor investigation quality. It reflects the limitations of the available evidence.

---

# Investigation Quality

An investigation is considered complete when there is sufficient evidence to support a conclusion or sufficient evidence to justify escalation.

Investigations are not expected to answer every question.

The objective is to make the best possible decision using the information available at the time.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Each completed investigation provides an opportunity to improve BluePeak's monitoring, processes and documentation.

Lessons learned are incorporated into future investigations where appropriate.
