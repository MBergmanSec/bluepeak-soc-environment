# Investigation Checklist

Use this checklist before closing or escalating a BluePeak security investigation.

## Context

- [ ] What triggered the investigation is understood
- [ ] Affected user, account or system identified
- [ ] Relevant business context considered
- [ ] Initial severity reviewed

## Evidence

- [ ] Relevant telemetry reviewed
- [ ] Important timestamps recorded
- [ ] Evidence correlated where appropriate
- [ ] Legitimate explanations considered
- [ ] Facts separated from assumptions

## Assessment

- [ ] Most likely explanation documented
- [ ] Business impact considered
- [ ] Confidence level assigned
- [ ] Important uncertainty clearly stated

## Outcome

- [ ] Final decision supported by evidence
- [ ] Required actions or recommendations recorded
- [ ] Escalation performed where appropriate
- [ ] Another analyst could understand the investigation
